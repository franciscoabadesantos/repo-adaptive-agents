# Methodology summary

The development experiment used ten cases, four synthetic repositories, and
fourteen organizational resources. It froze corpus membership and compared
tag, lexical, deterministic, direct-model, and hybrid conditions.

The blind holdout used 26 cases and repeated model conditions. Its public
protocol fixed retrieval and prompt construction. Pre-gold diagnostics covered
selection stability, exposure size, context, and protocol anomalies; semantic
success required independent private scoring.

Full inputs, runners, raw outputs, predictions, manifests, and authored gold
are kept in restricted archive units named in the artifact registry.
